About Scamproof

Scamproof is an offline conversation trainer for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Explore 30 fictional scenarios, choose a reply, use Pause & think for a hint, and reflect on the outcome. The Field Guide contains lessons and useful phrases; Journey shows your practice history and saved lessons.

Common questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. All practices and lessons are included in the app. No internet connection, account, subscription, or purchase is needed to use them. Sending a support email or visiting these pages requires a connection.

Does Scamproof check real messages or block scams?

No. It uses written, branching simulations. It does not scan your device, inspect messages, contact other people, make payments, or use live AI. Practice is preparation, not a guarantee of protection. The app and its support email are not an emergency or fraud-reporting service.

How do I continue an unfinished practice?

Return to Practice and resume your session. Choices and hints are saved as you go. There is one unfinished practice at a time. Starting a different one offers Resume or Replace practice; replacing it keeps completed history. Choosing Finish early saves a partial reflection.

Does rewinding change my results?

Rewind this moment lets you explore another response. Replay is temporary: it does not change the original attempt or add completion credit.

How do I delete my progress?

Open the app’s settings (“Your room”), tap Delete all progress, and confirm. This removes practice history, the unfinished session, and saved lessons. Your haptics preference remains. Deletion cannot be undone in the app.

Will my progress sync to another device?

Scamproof has no account or cloud sync. System backups may contain app data, depending on your settings. We cannot remotely recover local progress. Deleting and reinstalling the app may remove it, so do not use reinstallation as a first troubleshooting step.

What if a practice will not load or save?

Use Retry if it is offered. Check that your device has available storage, then close and reopen the app. A failed save keeps the conversation at its last saved step. If the problem continues, contact support with the exact error and practice title before deleting any data.

How do I turn off vibrations?

Open “Your room” and switch off Gentle haptics. Sound is never required.
